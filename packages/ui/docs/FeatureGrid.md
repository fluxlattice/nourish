# FeatureGrid

Three-across row of capability labels on the welcome card, set in stamp-red script.

```jsx
<FeatureGrid labels={["Goal-based", "Budget-aware", "Diet-friendly"]} />
```

**Text only** — there are no icons, boxes or tiles behind these. They read as three things jotted across the card, which is why they carry no border. An earlier version had bordered tiles with emoji and they were deliberately removed.

Each label is a noun phrase of two or three words; there's no room for more.
