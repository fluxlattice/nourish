# StepSub

The explanatory line under a `StepTitle`, set on the card's ruled lines.

```jsx
<StepTitle>About you</StepTitle>
<StepSub>Used to calculate your ideal calorie targets.</StepSub>
```

Its line-height is the rule spacing (`--rule-line`), so text sits **on** the blue rules instead of floating between them. That alignment is the whole trick of the index-card look — never override the line-height.

Says *why* the app is asking. One or two sentences.
